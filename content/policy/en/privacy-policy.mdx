**Last updated:** February 1, 2026

At **alckor.dev** we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and what your rights are.

## 1. Information We Collect

| Data                                  | Purpose                                        | Source            | Legal Basis         |
| ------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | ----------------- | ------------------- |
| **Email** (optional, newsletter only) | Send you blog updates via email                | Directly from you | Consent             |
| **Anonymous browsing data**           | Web analytics: page views, performance, errors | Vercel Analytics  | Legitimate interest |

### Newsletter (Mailchimp)

If you subscribe to our newsletter, your email is stored in **Mailchimp** to send you periodic updates about new articles and blog content. You can unsubscribe at any time by clicking the "unsubscribe" link included in every email.

### Web analytics (Vercel Analytics and Speed Insights)

We use **Vercel Analytics** and **Vercel Speed Insights** to collect anonymous performance and usage metrics:

- Pages visited, load times, errors
- Approximate country/region (without personal identification)
- Device type and browser

This data **does not** include your full IP address or information that personally identifies you. Vercel complies with GDPR and processes data anonymously.

## 2. How We Use Your Data

- **Newsletter**: Send emails with new articles and technical content. We never share your email with third parties or send advertising unrelated to the blog.
- **Analytics**: Measure site performance, understand which content is most useful, and detect technical issues.

**We do not sell, rent, or share** your personal information with third parties for commercial purposes.

## 3. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your data under the following legal bases (GDPR):

- **Consent**: When you voluntarily subscribe to the newsletter (you can revoke your consent at any time).
- **Legitimate interest**: For anonymous web analytics that allows us to improve the site and ensure its proper functioning.

## 4. Cookies

**alckor.dev** uses the following cookies:

| Cookie                   | Purpose                                   | Type       | Duration                        |
| ------------------------ | ----------------------------------------- | ---------- | ------------------------------- |
| `theme` (localStorage)   | Remember your light/dark theme preference | Functional | Permanent (until you delete it) |
| Vercel Analytics cookies | Anonymous performance telemetry           | Analytics  | Session/12 months               |

**We do not use** advertising or third-party cookies for tracking or marketing purposes.

You can configure your browser to reject cookies, although this may affect site functionality (for example, your theme preference will not be saved).

## 5. Data Retention

- **Newsletter**: We retain your email while you maintain an active subscription. If you unsubscribe, Mailchimp removes your email from our list.
- **Analytics**: Vercel retains anonymous data according to its retention policy (typically up to 12 months).

## 6. Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are in the European Union or jurisdictions with similar data protection laws, you have the right to:

- **Access**: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
- **Rectification**: Correct inaccurate data.
- **Erasure** ("right to be forgotten"): Request deletion of your data.
- **Object**: Object to processing based on legitimate interest.
- **Portability**: Receive your data in a structured format.
- **Withdraw consent**: Cancel your subscription at any time.

To exercise these rights, write to **alckordev@gmail.com**. We will respond within a maximum of 30 days.

You can also file a complaint with the data protection authority in your country.

## 7. Security

We employ industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

- **HTTPS**: All communication with the site is encrypted.
- **Trusted providers**: Mailchimp and Vercel comply with security standards (GDPR, SOC 2, ISO 27001).
- **Limited access**: Only authorized personnel can access newsletter data.

However, no system is 100% secure. If you detect any vulnerability, report it to **alckordev@gmail.com**.

## 8. International Transfers

- **Mailchimp** (owned by Intuit) stores data on servers primarily located in the United States. Mailchimp complies with the **EU-US Data Privacy Framework** and uses **Standard Contractual Clauses** (SCC) to ensure an adequate level of protection.
- **Vercel Inc.** (United States) processes anonymous data and complies with GDPR through standard contractual clauses.

Both providers ensure that data from European citizens is protected in accordance with EU privacy regulations.

## 9. Children

**alckor.dev** does not knowingly collect information from children under 13 years of age (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction). If you discover that a minor has provided their email in the newsletter, contact us to remove it immediately.

## 10. External Links

The blog may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites. We recommend reading their privacy policies.

## 11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We will post any changes on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Continued use of the site after such changes constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.

## 12. Contact

If you have questions, concerns, or requests related to your privacy, write to:

**Email:** alckordev@gmail.com

We will respond as soon as possible.
